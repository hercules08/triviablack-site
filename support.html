Your account

I played years ago. Is my progress still there?

Yes. Your coins, level, power-ups, Ad-Free and single-player high score carried over to the new app.

Tap Have an account? Log In and enter the same email address you used before. If the app asks you to choose a username, that email isn't on an account yet, so go back and try another one.

Games that were still in progress in the old app didn't carry over. Start a new one anytime.

Can't remember which email you used? Email us the name you played under and we'll help you find your account.

I forgot my password
  1. Tap Have an account? Log In, enter your email address and tap NEXT.
  2. Tap Forgot password?, then CONFIRM.
  3. We'll email you a six-digit code. Enter it in the app with your new password.

The code works for 15 minutes, so keep the app open until you've used it.

No email? Check your spam, junk or promotions folder for "Trivia Black Password Reset" from contact@triviablack.com. If it still hasn't arrived, or you can no longer get into that email account, email us with your username.

I used to log in with Facebook

Facebook login isn't available right now, but your account and progress are safe.

To get back in, tap Have an account? Log In, enter the email address on your account, then use Forgot password? to set a password (see I forgot my password).

Not sure which email is on your account, or never set one? Email us the name you played under and we'll help.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, tap your profile picture on the home screen to open Settings, then tap Delete Account.

Can't get into the app? Follow the steps on our account deletion page.

Deleting is permanent. Coins, purchases and progress can't be restored afterwards.

Purchases

How do I get Ad-Free back on a new phone?

Ad-Free is saved to your Trivia Black account, not to your phone. Install Trivia Black on the new phone and log in to the same account, and Ad-Free comes back on its own. This works when you switch between iPhone and Android too.

Still seeing ads? Email us your username and your App Store or Google Play receipt.

What is the Deck Pass?

The Deck Pass unlocks every premium topic deck for 30 days. It costs 300 coins (in-game coins, not money) and doesn't renew automatically.

To get it, go to the single-player topic screen, tap any locked Premium Topic and choose GET FOR 300. Buying it again while it's active adds another 30 days.

Bought the premium topic in the old app? You kept premium access, with no expiry.

Purchases and refunds

Payments go through Apple or Google, so they handle refunds. We can't issue them ourselves.

  • iPhone or iPad: request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Android: see Google Play's refund help.

Paid, but your coins or Ad-Free didn't show up? Close and reopen the app while logged in to the same account. Trivia Black finishes interrupted purchases when you sign in. If it's still missing, email us your username and your receipt from Apple or Google.

Other players

How do I report a player?

If a player's username or photo is offensive, or someone is harassing you, email us with their username and what happened. A screenshot helps.

We review reports within 24 hours and can remove a username or photo, or suspend the account.
