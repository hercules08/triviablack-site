Delete your account

Trivia Black by Swyft Studios LLC

You can delete your Trivia Black account and its data at any time, either in the app or by sending us a request.

Option 1: In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Trivia Black and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile picture (the gear icon) to open Settings.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. If you signed up with email, re-enter your password.

Your account is deleted immediately and you're signed out.

Option 2: Send us a request

If you can't access the app, email us from the address on your account (or include your username) and we'll delete the account within 30 days. We may reply to confirm that the account is yours.

Email a deletion request

Or write to contact@triviablack.com with the subject "Account deletion request".

What gets deleted

DeletedKept (and why)
  • Email address, username, display name and password
  • Profile photo
  • Coins, level, power-ups, turns and other progress
  • Push-notification token and scheduled notifications
  • Your feedback, question ratings and reports
  • Facebook link (if you used Facebook Login)
  • Games against other players stay in their history but are shown as "Deleted player" and are no longer linked to you.
  • Purchase records are kept, de-identified, where needed for accounting, refunds, tax or legal obligations.
  • Residual copies in backups are removed on our normal backup rotation.
Deleting your account can't be undone. Coins, purchases and progress can't be restored afterwards. To stop being charged for anything, manage purchases in your App Store or Google Play account. Trivia Black has no subscriptions.

Facebook Login users

Deleting your account removes the Facebook ID, name, email and picture we received from Facebook. You can also remove Trivia Black's access in Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites.
