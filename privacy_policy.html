Trivia Black

Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

Trivia Black is a head-to-head trivia game published by Swyft Studios LLC ("Swyft Studios", "we", "us"). This policy explains what information we collect when you use the Trivia Black apps for iOS and Android and this website, how we use and share it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, email contact@triviablack.com.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address, username and display name, and a password (stored only as a salted hash). If you sign in with Facebook, we receive your Facebook user ID, name, email address and profile picture from Facebook.
  • Profile photo (optional): a picture you choose to upload. It's shown to other players.
  • Feedback and reports (optional): messages you send us, question ratings, and reports about incorrect questions.

Information created when you play

  • Gameplay: your games and opponents, answers, scores, level, coins, power-ups, turns and rankings.
  • Purchases: when you buy coins, ad-free play or premium content, Apple or Google processes the payment. We receive a purchase record (product, transaction ID and receipt) to deliver the item and prevent fraud. We never receive your card or bank details.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and app identifiers: a push-notification token (if you allow notifications), an app-instance identifier used for analytics, and your device's advertising identifier (if your device settings allow it).
  • Usage and diagnostics: which screens you use and in-app events, crash and performance information, app version, device model and operating system.
  • Approximate location: our analytics and advertising providers may estimate a general location (such as city or region) from your IP address. We don't collect precise location.

How we use information

  • To create and run your account, match you with opponents, keep score and show your profile to other players.
  • To deliver purchases and rewards, and to detect fraud, cheating and abuse.
  • To send game notifications (for example "it's your turn") if you turn them on, and to send account emails such as password-reset codes.
  • To understand how the game is used, fix problems and improve questions and features.
  • To show advertising that keeps the game free (players who buy ad-free play don't see ads).
  • To respond to your messages and to comply with law.

How we share information

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only as follows:

  • With other players: your username, display name, profile photo, level and game results are visible to players you play with and in rankings.
  • With service providers that process data for us under our instructions: our hosting infrastructure; Google Firebase (analytics and push notifications); Mailchimp Transactional / Mandrill (account emails); and Apple and Google (app distribution and payments).
  • With advertising partners: we use Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, approximate location, app interactions and diagnostics to serve, measure and protect ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • With Facebook, only if you choose Facebook Login, to authenticate you. We don't send Facebook your gameplay data, and the app doesn't log app events or your advertising identifier to Facebook.
  • For legal reasons: to comply with law or valid legal process, to protect the rights, safety and property of our players or others, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets (with notice to you).

Advertising choices

  • iOS: the app asks for permission before your advertising identifier can be used for tracking. You can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads).
  • Buying ad-free play removes ads from the game.

Children

Trivia Black is intended for players aged 13 and older and isn't directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep account information while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the app: Settings → Delete Account.
  • On the web: follow the steps on our account deletion page.

When you delete your account, we delete your account details, profile photo, push token, feedback and other personal data. Games you played against other people stay in their history but are shown as "Deleted player" and are no longer linked to you. We keep de-identified purchase records where needed for accounting, refunds, tax or legal obligations. Residual copies in backups are removed on our normal backup rotation.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords are stored as salted hashes, and access to our systems is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a unique password.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example California or the European Economic Area), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. You can do most of this in the app. For anything else, email contact@triviablack.com. We'll respond within the time the law requires and won't discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

International users

We're based in the United States, and our systems and service providers may process information in the United States and other countries.

Changes to this policy

We'll post any changes on this page and update the effective date. If the changes are significant, we'll let you know in the app.
