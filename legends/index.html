Think you know them?

Four questions from Black Tech Legends. Pick your answer, then tap to check it.

  1. Pioneers

    John Glenn asked NASA to have one mathematician double-check the electronic computer’s numbers before his 1962 orbital flight. Who was it?

    • Mary Jackson
    • Dorothy Vaughan
    • Christine Darden
    • Katherine Johnson
    Tap to reveal the answerHide the answer
    Katherine Johnson

    NASA’s biography says Glenn wanted her to check the computer’s orbital numbers by hand before he would fly.

  2. Pioneers

    Mathematician Gladys West’s modeling of the Earth’s shape helped make which everyday technology possible?

    • Touchscreens
    • Bluetooth
    • GPS
    • Wi-Fi
    Tap to reveal the answerHide the answer
    GPS

    Her satellite-data model of the Earth’s shape underpinned the calculations that made GPS possible and accurate.

  3. Pioneers

    Jerry Lawson led the Fairchild team behind the Channel F (1976), the first home video game system built around what?

    • Wireless controllers
    • Online multiplayer
    • Programmable game cartridges
    • A touchscreen
    Tap to reveal the answerHide the answer
    Programmable game cartridges

    Lawson headed engineering at Fairchild, and his team built the first cartridge-based home video game system.

  4. Pioneers

    In 1969, Clarence “Skip” Ellis became the first African American to earn a PhD in which field?

    • Physics
    • Computer science
    • Chemistry
    • Mathematics
    Tap to reveal the answerHide the answer
    Computer science

    He earned it at the University of Illinois, then went on to build early office software at Xerox PARC.
